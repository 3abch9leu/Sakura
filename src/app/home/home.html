03 — Elements

Four ideas,
zero wires.

No power. Just gravity.

Water flows from a raised reservoir through gravity channels to wicks at each plant. No pump, no sensors, no Wi-Fi.

Shade that follows the sun.

Openings are tuned by sun-path simulation for your orientation, so light is filtered, not just blocked.

Edible by design.

Pockets for mint, basil and rosemary, fed by a water budget that always puts plants first.

Heritage, computed.

Qallaline and asanoha become parametric geometry, blended under engineering constraints and always credited to their traditions.
