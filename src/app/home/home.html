The principle

AI designs it. Nature powers it. Life fills the gaps.

A thin tube brings water in from above. Channels hidden inside the timber frame carry it down by gravity to every planted cell, where it is stored in a compartment under the roots: no pump, no tank on top. The open cells filter the sun and let the wall breathe, and the plants grow inside the geometry itself.

The prototype

An artificial root system. A living architecture.

An asanoha lattice whose empty spaces become growing cells. Water enters through one tube at the top, falls through channels hidden in the struts and is stored in the floor beam of each planted cell. Drag to turn it, then switch between architecture, planting, water, sun and the exploded view.
